LEALLA is described in Mao and Nakagawa, Learning Lightweight Language-agnostic
Sentence Embeddings with Knowledge Distillation, EACL 2023.
This PyTorch model is a conversion of the original TensorFlow LEALLA model.

DeedWise preparation preserves the BERT pooler sentence representation, adds
an identity-initialized projection adapter, exports ONNX, quantizes weights,
and precomputes the 50-deed catalog's reference embeddings.
